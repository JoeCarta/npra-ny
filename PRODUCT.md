# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Puerto Ricans and allies across New York State: community members, grassroots organizers, prospective members and volunteers, and partner organizations and civic/government contacts. Many are bilingual (English/Spanish); a meaningful share are older or less tech-fluent. They arrive wanting to understand who NPRA-NY is, see that it's a serious and rooted coalition, and find a clear way to join the work.

The primary job: turn an interested visitor into a member or volunteer. Secondary jobs: establish credibility with partners and government, surface current campaigns (housing, the Esencia opposition, disaster relief), and keep the community informed through events and La Agenda Nuevayol (the newsletter).

## Product Purpose

NPRA-NY is the New York State Chapter of the National Puerto Rican Agenda, a nonpartisan coalition-building alliance founded in 2017. It unites statewide organizations, community leaders, and grassroots organizers to advance economic, educational, healthcare, and housing equity for Puerto Rican communities across New York.

The site is the chapter's public face and its front door for participation. Success is a visitor who leaves understanding the mission and takes a concrete step: becoming a member, volunteering, attending an event, or subscribing to the newsletter.

## Brand Personality

Proud, rooted, dignified. The voice carries cultural pride and heritage with gravitas, not flash, and not kitsch. Warm and grounded, but serious enough that coalitions and government take it seriously. It speaks plainly, in the community's own voice (bilingual register welcome), and lets the work and the people carry the weight rather than slogans.

## Anti-references

- **Generic nonprofit template.** The stock-photo, blue-and-gray, interchangeable charity look. Forgettable and institutional.
- **Cold corporate / SaaS.** Startup landing-page energy: gradient hero metrics, sterile cards, anything that reads as disconnected from a real community.
- **Partisan / political-campaign aesthetics.** NPRA-NY is nonpartisan; it must not look like a candidate's election site.
- **Cliché flag-and-coquí kitsch.** Heritage carried by typography, imagery, copy, and the people, not by plastering the Puerto Rican flag everywhere as the entire identity.

## Design Principles

- **Heritage is earned, not decorated.** Cultural identity comes through real imagery, real voices, and typographic confidence, not flag motifs or stock symbolism.
- **Dignity over spectacle.** Gravitas and restraint signal a serious institution; energy is expressed through clarity and craft, not noise.
- **Every page points to joining.** Recruitment is the throughline; the path to membership/volunteering is always visible and never more than a step away.
- **Show the work and the people.** Concrete campaigns, initiatives, events, and the board build credibility better than mission abstractions.
- **Built for everyone in the community.** Bilingual-friendly, legible for older and less tech-fluent visitors, accessible by default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target **WCAG 2.1 AA** throughout: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, including placeholder and muted text. The current dark theme leans on low-opacity white (`rgba(255,255,255,0.5–0.68)`) for secondary text; audit these against AA and bump toward the ink end where they fall short.
- **Bilingual (EN/ES):** copy should read naturally to a bilingual audience, and layouts must tolerate longer Spanish strings without overflow or clipping (test headings and buttons at ES lengths).
- **Older / less technical users:** comfortable text sizes, generous tap targets, obvious affordances, simple flows.
- **Reduced motion:** every animation needs a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative (the site already does this in places; hold all new motion to the same bar).
